## Brand & Style
The design system reflects a premium, world-class enterprise technology consultancy. It bridges corporate reliability with modern digital agility, projecting deep technical expertise, institutional trust, and forward-looking innovation.

The aesthetic fuses modern corporate precision with high-end digital agency polish: expansive whitespace, deliberate typographic hierarchy, atmospheric cool-tinted surfaces, and sharp, structural micro-interactions. The visual environment avoids unnecessary decorative noise, letting content density, precise grid alignments, and refined surface layers evoke authority and scale.

## Colors
The palette balances authoritative deep navy with luminous technological blues:
- **Primary Navy (`#203F5F`)**: Applied to high-emphasis interactive states, brand marks, and prominent anchor headers.
- **Royal Blue (`#3979C7`)**: Functions as the primary interactive driver, active status indicators, and vibrant focus rings.
- **Sky Blue (`#50B5E5`)**: Utilized strategically for technological highlights, micro-badges, and visual data accents.
- **Neutral & Typography Layers**: Text defaults to deep midnight (`#172B42`) for WCAG AAA compliance on light surfaces, while secondary messaging utilizes slate gray (`#66788A`).
- **Surface Foundations**: Base surfaces remain clean white (`#FFFFFF`), while tiered containers leverage pale ice tints (`#F2F7FC` and `#E8F2FB`) to subtly delineate sections without heavy division lines.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm combines geometric, humanized structural headers (**Plus Jakarta Sans**) with systematic, neutral readability (**Inter**) for data and body copy.

- **Headlines**: Set tightly with negative letter-spacing for large display contexts to preserve visual punch and avoid ragged silhouettes.
- **Labels & Action Anchors**: Capitalize on Plus Jakarta Sans' sturdy geometric x-height to maintain legibility at 12px-14px sizes in interactive buttons, metadata tags, and metric indicators.
- **Body Copy**: Standard line height is set generously (1.6x) in Inter to support sustained technical reading across case studies, solution briefs, and data sheets.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-responsive 12-column grid constrained to an expansive 1280px max-width container for desktop presentations, stepping down to 8 columns on tablet devices and 4 columns on mobile viewports.

- **Vertical Rhythm**: Large section divisions leverage `space-2xl` (64px) to create breathable air between solution pillars, client proofs, and value propositions.
- **Micro-alignment**: Internal container padding enforces consistent horizontal baselines. Card grids enforce an equal-height flexbox or grid matrix with uniform `space-lg` (24px) gutters.
- **Reflow Logic**: On screens narrower than 768px, multi-column card groups break gracefully into single-column vertical stacks with `space-md` gaps.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is expressed through subtle tonal shifts and luminous ambient shadows rather than heavy structural drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Inset)**: Base white background (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted canvas (`#F2F7FC`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Default)**: Crisp 1px solid perimeter outline in `#E2EBF5`, paired with an ultra-soft blue-tinted drop shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(32, 63, 95, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(32, 63, 95, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active Focus)**: Subtle vertical lift (-3px translate), border shifts to `#3979C7` at 40% opacity, and shadow blooms to `0 12px 30px -4px rgba(32, 63, 95, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(32, 63, 95, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Flyouts)**: Navigation panels, modals, and tooltips cast `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(23, 43, 66, 0.12)`.

## Shapes
A unified rounded aesthetic is applied across containers and interactive components:
- Standard structural cards, dashboards, and media wrappers implement a disciplined 14px to 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg`), softening the corporate landscape while preserving architectural order.
- Action items (buttons, search inputs, dropdowns) use matching rounded edges (8px to 12px) to communicate clean ergonomics.
- Badges and micro-status tags adopt continuous full-radius pill styling.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#203F5F` fill, pure white text, 48px to 54px height, 10px corner radius, horizontal padding 28px. Subtle gradient highlight overlay on hover (`#3979C7`).
- **Secondary**: Transparent background with a 1.5px solid `#3979C7` outline, `#203F5F` text, shifting to `#F2F7FC` background fill on hover.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Zero outline, `#3979C7` text with an animated 2px underline transition on hover.

### Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#E2EBF5` border, and 16px corner radius.
- Padding sits at 32px (`space-xl`) desktop, 20px mobile. Hover interactions include slight elevation blooming and seamless icon color transitions from `#203F5F` to `#3979C7`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form controls maintain a generous 48px height with 1px solid `#E2EBF5` borders, smooth 8px radius, and `#172B42` text.
- Focus states invoke a crisp `0 0 0 3px rgba(57, 121, 199, 0.20)` ring and transition the border color to `#3979C7`.
- Checkboxes and radio buttons employ a custom 20px structural square/circle with `#3979C7` solid fill when checked, accompanied by high-contrast white glyph marks.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped with 4px vertical by 12px horizontal padding.
- Light tint variations use `#E8F2FB` background with `#203F5F` or `#3979C7` typography for solution categories, cloud competencies, and metadata filters.

### Enterprise Solution Metric Display
- Dedicated corporate KPI containers with huge numerical anchors (`headline-xl` in `#203F5F`) paired with clean `#66788A` secondary descriptions and a 3px vertical accent bar in `#50B5E5`.
